# Digital Privacy & Account Hardening Guide

Proactive steps to safeguard your online presence and mitigate targeting.

## 1. Profile Visibility
- Switch profiles to **Private**: Ensure only approved followers can view your stories, posts, and followers list.
- Turn off **Active Status**: Disabling "Show Activity Status" prevents harassers from timing their messages when you are online.

## 2. Direct Message Filters
- Instagram: Settings -> Privacy -> Messages -> Set "Others on Instagram" to "Don't receive requests".
- Lock down tag and mention permissions: Restrict "Who can tag you" and "Who can mention you" to "People You Follow".

## 3. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- Enable App-based 2FA (e.g. Google Authenticator or hardware keys) on email and social media to prevent account takeover.

## 4. Personal Information Hygiene
- Remove phone numbers, school/work locations, and mutual friend tags from public bios.
