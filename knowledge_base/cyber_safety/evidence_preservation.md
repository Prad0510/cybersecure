- **Source:** Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), Ministry of Home Affairs
- **URL:** https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/Crime_Manual.aspx
- **Retrieval Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Document Title:** Standard Operating Procedure for Electronic Evidence Preservation
- **Jurisdiction:** India (Information Technology Act, 2000 & Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023)
- **Language:** English

## 1. Preserving Digital Evidence

Before blocking, deleting, or confronting an abuser online, victims must preserve tamper-evident digital records:

1. **Full-Screen Uncropped Screenshots:**
   - Capture the entire device screen displaying the URL bar, timestamp, network status, battery indicator, and sender handle.
   - Do not crop or edit screenshots; cropping destroys evidential integrity.
2. **Metadata & Profile URLs:**
   - Record the perpetrator's exact profile URL (e.g., `instagram.com/username`), numerical User ID, and account creation date if visible.
3. **Message Headers & Raw Transcripts:**
   - On WhatsApp/Telegram: Export chat without media (`.txt` transcript).
   - On Email: Download full original email headers (RFC 822 format) containing sender IP and mail relay path.
4. **Certificate under Section 63 of BSA 2023 (formerly 65B of Indian Evidence Act):**
   - Retain the physical mobile phone or computer on which the communications were received in their original state.
