## 1. Applicable Sections of the Information Technology Act, 2000

- **Section 66E (Violation of Privacy):** Punishes intentional capturing, publishing, or transmitting images of a private area of any person without consent (imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to ₹2 lakh).
- **Section 67 (Obscenity in Electronic Form):** Punishes publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form.
- **Section 67A (Sexually Explicit Act):** Punishes transmitting sexually explicit acts or material (imprisonment up to 5 years for first conviction).
- **Section 67B (Child Exploitation):** Stringent penalties for child-related abuse, grooming, and exploitation online.

## 2. Applicable Provisions under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023

- **Section 78 (Stalking):** Monitoring a woman's internet, email, or electronic communication against her disinterest constitutes punishable cyberstalking.
- **Section 79 (Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman):** Covers vulgar slurs, morphing, and abusive online targeting.
- **Section 351 (Criminal Intimidation):** Threatening injury to person, reputation, or property via electronic messaging.
- **Section 356 (Defamation):** Defaming someone via online comments, memes, or public posts.
