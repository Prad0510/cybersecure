# Official Indian Cybercrime Reporting Procedures

Guidelines for registering formal complaints regarding online harassment, cyberbullying, morphed imagery, or threats.

## 1. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
- **Website**: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- **National Helpline Number**: **1930** (24x7 toll-free helpline across all States and Union Territories)
- **Filing Anonymous Complaints**: For cases involving child sexual abuse material or cyber-blackmail of women, complaints can be lodged anonymously.

## 2. Information Required for Complaint Filing
- Detailed chronology of the harassment.
- Suspect details (Profile handles, URLs, phone numbers, email addresses if known).
- Evidence files (Uncropped screenshots, PDF incident reports, audio/video recordings).
- Identity proof of complainant (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID).

## 3. Immediate Preservation Orders
- Under Section 91 of the CrPC / IT Act 2000, designated cyber cells can issue preservation requests to social media intermediaries to retain server IP logs and account records.
