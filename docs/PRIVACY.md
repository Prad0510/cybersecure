# CyberGuard Privacy & Ethical Principles

## 1. Ethical AI Boundaries
- **No Mental Health Diagnosis**: The Support AI assistant is hardcoded to never provide psychiatric or psychological diagnoses (e.g. "You have depression", "You are fine"). It functions exclusively as a cyber safety, de-escalation, and resource-navigation tool.
- **No Legal Declarations**: Cyberbullying analyses are presented as probabilistic language indicators (e.g. "Pattern of abusive language detected"), never definitive legal determinations of guilt or criminal conduct.

## 2. Data Minimization & Privacy Protection
- **Redaction in Logs**: Application logs never record full text transcripts of private chats, uploaded screenshot binaries, or personal identification fields.
- **Evidence Lifecycle & Deletion**: Users maintain sovereign control over their evidence. Incidents and attached screenshots can be permanently expunged upon user request.
- **Support Privacy**: Support sessions maintain rolling summary rollups rather than unencrypted long-term raw conversation dumps.
